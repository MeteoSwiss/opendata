## :recycle: Update interval for ground-based measurements' files with 10-minute values is set to 10 minutes

This performance improvement applies to the following datasets/collections:
- [Automatic weather stations](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/a-data-groundbased/a1-automatic-weather-stations#data-structure)
- [Automatic precipitation stations](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/a-data-groundbased/a2-automatic-precipitation-stations#data-structure)
- [Automatic tower stations](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/a-data-groundbased/a3-automatic-tower-stations#data-structure)

Note: It may take longer than 10 minutes for the values measured every 10 minutes to become available in the files.
